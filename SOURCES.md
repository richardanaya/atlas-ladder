# Sources

Atlas Ladder quotes only short passages, for study and criticism. Each quotation below is copied as a contiguous wording checked against the Ayn Rand Lexicon (aynrandlexicon.com). If a line is an excerpt from a longer sentence, the entry says so. Every quotation below is Ayn Rand’s. The citation names the work.

Page numbers for Galt’s speech follow *For the New Intellectual*, the pagination the Lexicon uses. The speech was written for *Atlas Shrugged* and is reprinted there.

Paraphrases in the app are labeled **Paraphrase** on the answer banner. They are not quotations and are not listed here.

The one-foot summary uses two familiar sayings as plain language. “Nature, to be commanded, must be obeyed” is Francis Bacon’s. “Give me liberty or give me death” is Patrick Henry’s. Rand offers them as translations of her branches. She did not originate them.

Do not treat this file as a license to copy the books. Read the works.
